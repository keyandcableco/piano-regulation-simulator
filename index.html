Moving around the action
  • Turn it: drag with the mouse.
  • Slide it: hold down Shift (or Ctrl) while you drag, or drag with the right mouse button.
  • Zoom: roll the mouse wheel.
  • Turn it: drag one finger.
  • Slide it: drag two fingers.
  • Zoom: pinch with two fingers.
  • Play the key: press and hold on the key itself.
  • Work on a part: click (or tap) it, a screw, a spring, the jack, for its tests and adjustments.
  • Or use the buttons on the right: + and − zoom and the arrows slide. Reset view puts it back if you get lost, and ? Help brings this back.
